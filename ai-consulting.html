What is ai consulting?

AI consulting is the process of finding where artificial intelligence can save time, cut cost or create revenue in your business, and planning how to build it safely. At JMINT we combine strategy with hands-on engineering, so every recommendation is something we have the skills to build and deliver. You get a practical AI strategy, not a slide deck that sits in a folder.

AI Consulting: your questions answered

What does an AI consultant do?

An AI consultant studies your business, finds where AI can save time or money, ranks those opportunities by return on investment and gives you a practical roadmap. At JMINT our consultants also build the solutions, so the plan is always realistic.

How long does an AI consulting engagement take?

A readiness assessment and roadmap usually takes one to three weeks depending on the size of your business. You get a fixed timeline and price before we begin.

Is AI consulting affordable for small and mid-size companies?

Yes. We offer fixed-scope, budget-friendly packages and start with quick wins, so you see returns early instead of paying for a big programme up front.

Do you only advise, or can you also build the solution?

Both. You can take our roadmap to any team, or let us build it. Many clients continue with us for AI agent, chatbot or automation development.
