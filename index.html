Model Evaluation Harness for a Customer-Support Assistant

EvalsRegression testsLLM-as-judge
The challenge
Every prompt change felt like a gamble because nobody knew whether answers had improved or quietly got worse.
What we built
We turned 400 real tickets into a scored test suite that runs on every change and compares models on accuracy, tone, refusal behaviour and cost per answer.
The outcome
Releases now ship with a one-page comparison, and the team picked a cheaper model after the numbers showed no quality loss.

Can you build an AI agent for accounting and finance teams?
