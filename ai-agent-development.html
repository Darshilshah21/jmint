AI Agents: your questions answered

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software powered by a large language model that can plan steps, use tools such as databases and APIs, and complete a task on its own while keeping a human in control of important decisions.

How is an AI agent different from a chatbot?

A chatbot mainly answers questions. An agent also takes actions – it can analyse files, update records, send emails or run a multi-step process to reach a goal.

How much does AI agent development cost?

Cost depends on the workflow and integrations. We scope every project with a fixed price, milestone payments and a working prototype early, so there are no surprises.

How long does it take to build a custom AI agent?

A focused agent is usually delivered in two to six weeks, starting with a working prototype in the first week or two.

Can an AI agent work with confidential financial data?

Yes. We use role-based access, encryption and private deployment options, and design agents to keep every insight traceable to its source data.
