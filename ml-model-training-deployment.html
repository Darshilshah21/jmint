What is model training?

Model work has three parts that are often confused. Training adapts a model to your data. Evaluation proves whether it actually got better. Deployment keeps it fast, cheap and monitored once real users arrive. We treat evaluation as the centre of the project, because a model nobody can measure is a model nobody can trust.

Model Training: your questions answered

Do I need fine-tuning or is RAG enough?

Start with retrieval and good prompts. Fine-tune when you need a fixed output style, domain vocabulary or lower cost at high volume. We run a small comparison first so you do not pay for training you do not need.

What hardware is needed to run a model locally?

A 7 to 8 billion parameter model runs well on a single 16 to 24 GB GPU once quantised. Larger models need more memory. We size this during the audit.

How do you prove a model is better?

With a test set built from your real cases that stays untouched during training, scored automatically and checked by a person on a sample.
