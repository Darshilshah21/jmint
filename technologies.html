Our delivery method

Six stages. Every one ends with something you can see, test or sign off.

1. Discover

Interviews, process mapping and data review. Output: a plain-language brief and a list of risks.

2. Size the value

We estimate hours saved, errors avoided or revenue gained, and compare it with build and running cost.

3. Prototype

A working slice in days, tested with your own data and your own people.

4. Build in sprints

Two-week cycles with a demo at the end of each one and a visible backlog.

5. Evaluate

Test sets, accuracy checks, security review and load tests before anything goes live.

6. Launch and support

Staged rollout, training, monitoring dashboards and a maintenance plan.
