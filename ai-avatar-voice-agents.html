What is ai avatars?

A live AI avatar combines speech recognition, a language model, text-to-speech and a rendered face that moves in sync with the audio. The hard part is not the face. It is keeping the delay under a second, letting the visitor interrupt, and making sure the avatar only says things your business can stand behind. We handle that full loop, from microphone to lip-sync.

AI Avatars: your questions answered

How fast can a live AI avatar respond?

With streaming recognition and speech output, most replies begin in well under two seconds. The exact figure depends on the language model you choose and the network at the venue, so we measure it on your site during the pilot.

Can the avatar use my own face or voice?

Yes, with written consent from the person. We also offer licensed stock characters if you prefer not to use a real likeness.

Will it run without internet?

A kiosk can run speech and language models locally on a GPU machine for sites with poor connectivity. Quality and cost trade-offs are explained in the proposal.
