AI Automation: your questions answered

What is AI automation?

AI automation uses artificial intelligence together with workflow software to handle tasks that need understanding, such as reading documents or writing replies, in addition to simple rule-based steps.

Which processes are best to automate first?

Start with high-volume, repetitive tasks that follow a clear pattern, such as data entry, document processing, reporting and email triage. We rank them during a short discovery session.

How much can I save with automation?

Savings depend on volume, but most clients target several hours per person per week. We estimate the return before we start so you can see the business case.

Will automation work with the software we already use?

Almost always. We integrate through APIs, connectors or, when needed, screen-level automation.
