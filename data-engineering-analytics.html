What is data & analytics?

Data engineering moves and organises data so it is complete, correct and on time. Analytics turns that data into decisions. We do both, and we build the foundation so that the same tables feed your dashboards today and your AI features tomorrow.

Data & Analytics: your questions answered

Do we need a data warehouse or is Excel enough?

If your reports take more than a few hours to assemble or numbers disagree between teams, a warehouse pays back quickly. We often start small with PostgreSQL or DuckDB and grow only if needed.

Which dashboard tool do you use?

Whichever fits your team. Power BI for Microsoft shops, Metabase or Superset for open-source setups, or a custom Django dashboard when you need your own logins and branding.

Can you work with our existing data analyst?

Yes. We often build the pipelines and models and hand the analysis layer to your team with documentation and training.
