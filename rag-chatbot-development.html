RAG Chatbots: your questions answered

What is a RAG chatbot?

A RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) chatbot looks up relevant passages in your own documents or data first, then uses an AI model to write the answer from those passages. This keeps answers accurate and lets the bot cite its sources.

How do you stop the chatbot from making things up?

We ground every answer in retrieved sources, set confidence thresholds, show citations and let the bot say it does not know. We also test with real questions before launch.

Can the chatbot use live data and not just documents?

Yes. Live-resource chatbots call your APIs, databases or approved web sources at question time, so answers reflect the latest information.

How much does a custom AI chatbot cost?

We quote a fixed price after a short discovery call. Many document chatbots can be live within two to four weeks, and we keep infrastructure costs low by design.
